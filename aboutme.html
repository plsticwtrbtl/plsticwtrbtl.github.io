Hello! My name is Mathew Lemelson. I like listening to music, playing the piano, building models, and caring for my animals. I am 15 years old and I am a student at Skagit Valley College. I am in my second year of school, and I am aspiring to be an Electrical Engineering Major. Music has always been a part of my life, I love listening to a wide range of genres when I can. When I am not playing the piano, I will usually be carefully priming, painting, cutting, and assembling plastic models. I really like this hobby because watching the pile of plastic pieces turn into something you can hold is very cool. Outside of school and hobbies, I help take care of my farm. Between music, model building, and my farm animals, I like having lots of different activities. Looking forward, I am excited to keep furthering my education.
